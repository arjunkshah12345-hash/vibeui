## Design Context

### Users
Frontend engineers and designers assembling product UIs and marketing surfaces. They want copy-paste components that feel finished without fighting a heavy design system.

### Brand Personality
Warm · Precise · Crafted

Emotional goals: calm confidence and a little delight. Polish comes from spacing, depth and motion, not decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Warm minimalism with a point of view.** Bone canvas and stone dark mode, an editorial serif (Instrument Serif) against a clean grotesque (Geist), layered soft shadows for depth, and a single vermilion accent.

**References (steal principles, not clones):**
- **shadcn/ui**: own-the-code components, accessible defaults, composition
- **Origin UI / coss ui**: dense app-primitive catalog
- **Magic UI**: selective, tasteful motion
- **Linear / Vercel**: restraint, hairlines, typographic hierarchy

**Anti-references:** Material purple SaaS, neon glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids, broadsheet newspaper layouts, glow orbs, gradient-text everywhere.

**Theme:** Light-first with warm-stone dark mode (`.dark` on `<html>`; `.light` / `.dark` scope any subtree). Tokens live in `templates/vibeui.css` and nowhere else.

### Design Principles
1. **Tokens only**: no raw hex in components. Radii `sm 8 / md 12 / lg 16 / xl 22`, shadows `quiet / lift / pop`.
2. **Ink is primary, accent is meaning**: primary actions are ink; the vermilion accent marks focus, selection, progress and live state. Pastels are for status.
3. **Depth is subtle and layered**: a hairline plus a soft shadow, never a heavy drop shadow or glow.
4. **Motion is purposeful**: transform and opacity, spring only where it adds life, always safe under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Pointer effects write CSS variables instead of re-rendering.
5. **Own the source**: every component is one self-contained file with a one-line description comment.
6. **Signature pieces earn their weight**: carousel, dock, trading card and friends must feel inevitable, not demoware.
