## Inspiration and acknowledgements

No source code from the projects below was copied into VibeUI. They informed the *ideas*,
vocabulary or conventions, and deserve credit:

- **[shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com)** (MIT): the copy-owned distribution model, the `cva` + `cn()`
  pattern, and compound-component naming conventions such as `Card`/`CardHeader` and
  `Tabs`/`TabsList`.
- **[ObsidianUI](https://www.obsidianui.dev)** (MIT): the idea of a catalogue of expressive, animated
  "craft" components (dock, trading card, magnetic and hover effects).
- **[Magic UI](https://magicui.design)** (MIT): selective, tasteful motion components such as marquees
  and text reveals.
- **[Origin UI / coss ui](https://coss.com/ui)**: a dense catalogue of app primitives.
- **macOS Dock**: the magnification behavior of `Dock` is a common interaction pattern.

As a sanity check, same-named components in shadcn/ui, ObsidianUI and Magic UI were compared with
VibeUI's using an 8-token overlap measure; the overlap was limited to short, idiomatic Tailwind and
`cva` boilerplate. That is a mechanical comparison, not a legal opinion.
