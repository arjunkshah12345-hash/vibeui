## Intent

A 50-second launch film for VibeUI (open-source, copy-owned React + Tailwind components). Warm, precise, a little playful; typographic and motion-led, no narration. It opens on the site's own tagline and its signature "periodic table" of 104 components, then proves the three claims: you own the files, one token file retints everything, and motion + agent tooling are built in.

## Assets

- Fonts: Instrument Serif (display), Geist (UI), Geist Mono, taken from the site's own build (SIL OFL).
- Brand: the VibeUI "V" mark, drawn in SVG. Vermilion accent in light scenes; dark scenes are monochrome (the user does not want orange in dark mode).
- Audio: an original, procedurally synthesised music bed (96 BPM) and the bundled Pixabay-licensed SFX library.

## Customizations

- SFX on every beat and transition, scene cuts on bar lines (bar = 2.5s).
- No voiceover; kinetic typography carries the story.

- Autonomous run: the user asked for a finished video with SFX and motion; decisions are recorded here and in the delivery note.
