# Code of conduct

VibeUI is a small project that welcomes people of every background and experience level. We want contributing to feel good, and we want the community to be a place people are glad to be part of.

## Our standard

Be kind, assume good faith, and keep the focus on the work.

**Things we encourage**

- Welcoming newcomers and answering questions patiently.
- Giving specific, constructive feedback, and receiving it graciously.
- Crediting other people's ideas and work.
- Disagreeing about code and design without making it personal.

**Things we do not accept**

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments, in any form or in any space tied to the project.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Threats, doxxing, or publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressuring people to act.
- Submitting work you do not have the right to submit.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and restrict participation to protect the community. If you experience or see behavior that violates this code, report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab using **Report a vulnerability**, and mark the report as a conduct concern, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

Maintainers will respond with proportionate action, from a private note to a permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.
